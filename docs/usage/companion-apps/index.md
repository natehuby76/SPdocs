# Companion Apps

Use a companion app to access Galaxy from your phone. Available tools depend on your app, connection and StarPilot build.

- [iOS](./ios.md) — StarPilot BT by **natehuby76**.
- [Android](./android.md) — Galaxy by **dirwin31**.

## Pair your phone

While parked, open **Settings → Bluetooth → Pair phone** on the comma, then follow the pairing prompts in your companion app. If the pairing window has closed, open it again on the comma.

Phone pairing is separate from pairing an [audio accessory or controller](../../exclusive/bluetooth-controls.md) or setting up [remote access in the browser](../galaxy.md#pairing).

## Choose a connection

Bluetooth-only access supports basic controls and settings where available. Use Galaxy Wi-Fi or the same local network for camera images, recordings, replay and larger transfers. See [Local access in Galaxy](../galaxy.md#local-access) for browser-based setup.

For beta access and help trying new test features, [ask in Discord](https://firestar.link/discord).
