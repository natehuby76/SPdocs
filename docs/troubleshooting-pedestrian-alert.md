# Pedestrian alert troubleshooting

If your car displays a pedestrian alert warning, check the owner’s manual for the exact dashboard message. Use the service guidance for your vehicle’s model year and configuration before changing any fuse or wiring.

If the warning appeared after installing StarPilot hardware, [ask for support](./ticketsystem.md) with the vehicle year and trim, installed hardware, exact warning and when it appeared.
