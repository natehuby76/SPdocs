# Bluetooth Controls

StarPilot supports Bluetooth accessories on comma 3, 3X and 4. Pair a device for audio or map supported Bluetooth or USB controller buttons to **Quick Select** and driving-screen actions.

This has been tested with:

* PlayStation 5 controllers
* Xbox controllers
* Macropads
* Keyboards
* A mouse
* Several Bluetooth audio devices

A link to the one I've been using is [here](https://amzn.to/4AcegLv) (this is an affiliate
link; I get a small kickback if you decide to get one).

## Pairing and Audio in Galaxy

Open **Bluetooth** in Galaxy to turn Bluetooth on, scan, pair and connect, forget a pairing, select an audio device, or test audio. For Spotify Connect and Phone Audio, see [StarPort](./starport.md#other-phones-and-audio).

### Controller Buttons

Use local Galaxy with the vehicle off and open **Controller Buttons**.

1. Check that your Bluetooth or USB controller appears under **Attached Controllers**.
2. Turn on **Enable assigned controller buttons**.
3. Choose a slot under **Favorite Buttons** or select an action under **Action Buttons**.
   Tap **Learn Button**, then press the button on your controller.
4. Use **Test buttons (20s)** to check that Galaxy sees the button press without running
   its action.

## On-Device Bluetooth Management

Bluetooth management is also available on the comma. Controls and layout depend on the device.

The Bluetooth icon appears along the bottom of the Big UI home screen.
